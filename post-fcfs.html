Method

Rosters were pulled from the live MFL export on Wednesday 9 September at 4:45 a.m. ET, which includes every transaction through 11:50 p.m. Tuesday. Every player is priced in eSFL scoring from his 2025 league-scored per-game rate. A player with 14 or more games in 2025 keeps his rate; below that, the rate is regressed toward a positional baseline in proportion to the games he missed, then multiplied by his expected 2026 games. Rookies, retirees and players with no 2025 line carry a projection instead.

Each roster is then run through every legal 14-man lineup (QB 1-2 · RB 0-4 · WR 0-4 · TE 1-3 · PK 1 · IDP 5) and the best one is kept. The bar on each card is that lineup's projected points by position, so the widths are directly comparable between teams. Unlike Sunday's report card there is no "patched" column: every slot is now filled by the player actually in it, which is why teams that took a weaker free agent than a typical one lose ground against their Sunday line even though they made moves.

Projections are opinions. Treat ±100 on a season total as noise, and treat the gaps between teams inside a 60-point band as no gap at all. Every team was run through the same model, including the author's.

Thirteen of fourteen rosters are now legal. Aaron Donald, retired, is still on two rosters. Micah Parsons is listed IR-PUP with an October return; Josh Jacobs' suspension ends 27 September. Sixty-three transactions were processed on Tuesday, thirty-one of them in the first fifteen minutes.
