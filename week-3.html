If the season ended today One division winner on record, two BlackJack teams on total points

Western. Perdew Pulverizers and Texan Bruisers are both 3-0, and they play each other in Week 4. The division lead goes to the winner. That game decides more than one spot. If Perdew holds the record lead, the BlackJack berths by points today are Psycho Billiecats (624.53) and Frost Furious Flurries (600.12), with Texan fourth at 555.49. If Texan takes the division instead, Perdew's 679.07 walks into a BlackJack spot and pushes Frost out. Texan has the record; it has not had the points.

Eastern. Nobody is unbeaten. Igama Sweaty Palms, Rhodes Runners and D Crownholder are all 2-1. The points race is the tightest thing in the league: six Eastern teams sit inside 34 points, from Igama Sweaty Palms at 588.37 down to D Crownholder at 554.15. One good Sunday reorders the whole division.

What the September pages got wrong. Psycho Billiecats was ranked 14th of 14, alone at the bottom. They are 2nd in the league in points, and their 242.60 was the high score of Week 3. That is the biggest miss on the board and it isn't close. Perdew Pulverizers was graded a B in the middle pack; they lead the league by 55 points and have the best all-play record at 34-5. Igama Sweaty Palms was 11th and leads the East.

And in the other direction: Nachman Manning Face (4th preseason) is 12th in points, mostly because its quarterbacks have scored 24.4 combined. Houston Rams (3rd) sits 7th after a 260 opening week followed by 131 and 174. The same page had Frost Furious Flurries 1st; they are 3rd. Three weeks is 23% of the regular season; this is a check, not a verdict.
