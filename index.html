Method

Every drafted player was priced in eSFL scoring: his actual 2025 league-scored total from the MFL export, blended with a 2026 projection for role, health and age. Rookies and players who missed most of 2025 get a projection only. Each roster was then run through every legal 14-man lineup (QB 1-2 · RB 0-4 · WR 0-4 · TE 1-3 · PK 1 · IDP 5) and the best one kept.

Two numbers per team: as drafted, where an empty starting slot scores zero, and patched, where each empty IDP or PK slot is filled with a typical free agent. The bar on each card is the projected points of that best lineup by position. Injury flags are from the MFL report as of Sept 7. Projections are opinions; treat ±100 on a season total as noise. Every team was run through the same model, including the author's.

Ten of fourteen teams drafted fewer than the five IDP or one kicker they need to start Week 1. Parsons is listed IR-PUP with an Oct 11 return, not out for the season. Jacobs' suspension ends Sept 27. Donald is retired. Mahomes, Nabers, Kraft and Bosa all carry knee/ACL flags on the Sept 7 MFL report.
