🕯️ Week 7: what went bump in the night

D Crownholder d. Zima
233.11 – 132.59

The week's high score and a 100.52-point win, the second-biggest margin of the season. Joe Burrow (40.26) and Blake Cashman (36.4) were the top two scorers in the league this week, and both started for D. Zima has now lost six straight.

Zima is down to its last candle.

Frost d. Perdew
213.86 – 182.36

The first meeting of the two Western points leaders. Jaxon Smith-Njigba (31.5), Trey McBride (27.1) and Tee Higgins (26.8) won it for Frost, which got there despite starting Patrick Mahomes at −9.78. Perdew has lost two straight, and its 5.71-point lead in the West has turned into a 59.22-point deficit.

Perdew's running backs are still the scariest thing in the West: 528.5 points.

Fields d. RJ
210.87 – 183.92

Fields has won two straight after starting 1-4. RJ started two quarterbacks: Burrow scored 40.26 and Bryce Young scored −15.44. Together that was 24.82, and RJ left 66.0 points on the bench.

RJ has the fourth-best all-play record in the league and is 2-5. The ghost of bad luck has moved in.

Wallie's d. Houston
206.65 – 169.41

Wallie's has won four straight, with Dak Prescott scoring 30.82. They now lead the league in points by 7.97 and are alone at the top of the East.

Four straight. The Stars are burning brightest in the East.

Albany d. Psycho
199.69 – 162.66

Albany has won two straight. Psycho has lost two straight; Brock Purdy scored −3.19 and Psycho left 53.5 points on the bench.

Psycho is 97 points behind the last BlackJack spot. Its pulse is getting faint.

Texan d. Nachman
172.18 – 167.67

The closest game of the week, decided by 4.51. Texan started a kicker who scored −2 and still won, and is now 6-1. Nachman is 1-6.

Seven weeks in, and Texan is still walking under ladders without a scratch.

Rhodes d. Igama
181.48 – 99.05

Sweaty palms, indeed.

👻 Who's haunting the bracket One division winner on record, two BlackJack teams on total points

Western. Texan Bruisers is 6-1, a game ahead of Frost Furious Flurries (5-2), and holds the head-to-head tiebreak from Week 1. That puts Texan in as the division winner today. The two BlackJack spots go on points to Frost (1,453.85) and Perdew (1,394.63). RJ Griffins is first out at 1,330.35, 64.28 behind Perdew. Texan is fifth of seven in Western points (1,231.17), so the record is the only way in for them: if Frost catches them in the standings, Texan has to make up 99 points on RJ just to get back to the line.

Eastern. Wallie's Texas Stars is 5-2, alone in first and top of the league in points. The BlackJack spots go to Houston Rams (1,338.60) and Rhodes Runners (1,297.66). D Crownholder is 14.45 behind Rhodes, close enough to close in a single good week. Fields Fanatix is 46 back, Igama Sweaty Palms 68 back. Four East teams are 4-3, so the record race is still open behind Wallie's.
