If the season ended today One division winner on record, two BlackJack teams on total points

Western. Three teams are 4-1: Perdew Pulverizers, Frost Furious Flurries and Texan Bruisers. Texan has beaten both of the others (Frost in Week 1, Perdew in Week 4), so on the bylaws' head-to-head step Texan holds the division today. That sends Perdew (1,054.20) and Frost (1,048.49) to the BlackJack spots on points, 5.71 apart. Psycho Billiecats is first out at 994.13, 54 back. Texan is fifth in Western points at 921.57, so the record lead is the only door it has: if Perdew or Frost takes it back, Texan falls behind Psycho in the points line. Perdew and Frost have not played yet; they meet in Week 7.

Eastern. Five teams are 3-2: Wallie's Texas Stars, Houston Rams, Igama Sweaty Palms, Rhodes Runners and D Crownholder. Wallie's has the most points in the league (1,070.02) and Houston is second in the East (1,012.44), so those two hold the BlackJack spots unless one of them wins the division. If one does, the last spot goes to Igama at 957.85, 2.55 points ahead of Rhodes. Nachman Manning Face and Fields Fanatix are 1-4, two games back of the pack and 57-plus points behind it.
